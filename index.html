Florence
Early Renaissance
  • Giotto breaks from flat medieval style with emotion, weight, and human grief.
  • Masaccio adds mathematical perspective and depth in The Holy Trinity.
  • Donatello revives classical free-standing sculpture with David.
Florence to Rome
High Renaissance
  • Leonardo blends observation, anatomy, perspective, and psychology.
  • Michelangelo emphasizes the heroic human body in sculpture and fresco.
  • Raphael creates calm, balanced Madonnas and classical scenes.
Venice
Later Renaissance
  • After Rome's decline, Venice becomes a major artistic center.
  • Venetian art favors color, atmosphere, elaborate settings, and drama.
  • Giorgione's The Tempest and Veronese's Feast in the House of Levi show the shift.
